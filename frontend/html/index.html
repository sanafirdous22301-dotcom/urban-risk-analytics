October 2020 Cloudburst 81 Fatalities

Extreme monsoon deluge (324 mm in 24 hours). Catastrophic breach and inundation across the Musi River basin, Moosarambagh, Begumpet, LB Nagar, and Alwal colonies.

Dates: Oct 13–19, 2020 · Duration: 7 days
August 2000 Mega-Flood 240+ Fatalities

Catastrophic urban flood (241 mm rainfall). Over 75 urban tank bunds breached simultaneously, inundating the Musi River corridor, Chaderghat, and lowlands.

Dates: Aug 23–25, 2000 · Duration: 3 days
August 1978 Regional Basin Flood 44 Fatalities

Prolonged 21-day regional inundation across twin cities and wider catchment. Extensive disruption of transport corridors and agricultural lowlands.

Dates: Aug 01–21, 1978 · Duration: 21 days
September 1983 Flash Flood 12 Fatalities

Intense 24-hour localized downpour causing severe runoff congestion in central Hyderabad, Koti, and Old City drainage catchments.

Dates: Sep 23, 1983 · Duration: 1 day
